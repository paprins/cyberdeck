## Design Context

### Users
Primarily the device owner (technically capable) plus non-technical family members. Used in normal times for curiosity/prep, but critically in emergency / SHTF (Shit Hits The Fan) scenarios: power outages, natural disasters, grid-down situations. Device runs on battery. Users may be stressed, tired, or operating under duress. Clarity and speed of comprehension are life-critical.

### Brand Personality
Reliable. Utilitarian. Prepared.
Not sci-fi fantasy — grounded, tool-like, trustworthy. The aesthetic of a field manual, a cockpit instrument panel, or military-grade equipment. It should feel like something that *works when nothing else does*.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Dark. The device is used in low-light emergencies, conserves battery in dark mode, and the operator context (survival situations) calls for a serious, command-center feel.
- **Visual tone**: Military-utilitarian meets approachable family tool. Think: instrument panel clarity with enough warmth that a scared family member isn't intimidated.
- **References**: Aviation cockpit displays, field manuals, ruggedized military handhelds (like a Garmin inReach), emergency services dispatch screens.
- **Anti-references**: Anything that looks like a consumer app, a dashboard SaaS product, or glowing neon cyberpunk aesthetics.
- **Typography**: B612 (designed by Airbus for avionics/cockpit displays — optimized for critical readability at small sizes). Body: Atkinson Hyperlegible (designed for maximum legibility, including low-vision users under stress).
- **Color**: Very dark olive-black background (not blue-black — warmer, more grounded). Tile accents use muted, purposeful color — amber for medical (urgency + life), earth-green for maps, burnt orange for survival, olive for food, steel-blue for reference.

### Design Principles
1. **Clarity under stress** — hierarchy must be instantly obvious, even at a glance or under duress
2. **Touch-first, never tiny** — minimum 48px touch targets, preferably much larger for primary actions
3. **Power awareness** — no animations that burn CPU/GPU, no gradients that require compositing, no blur effects
4. **Tool, not toy** — earn trust through precision and restraint; avoid decorative elements that don't carry information
5. **Family-legible** — non-technical users should navigate confidently without instruction
